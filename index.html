Frequently Asked Questions

Bed Bug Eradication FAQs

Have questions about preparation, thermal heat vs chemical, or warranties? Find quick answers below.

Preparing for thermal heat treatment is straightforward but essential. You will need to remove heat-sensitive items (like candles, wax products, aerosols, carbonated cans, electronics with lithium-ion batteries, and oil paintings). All pets must be vacated, and clothing in chests of drawers should be left loosely spaced to let hot air penetrate. We provide a complete pre-treatment PDF checklist before our team arrives.
Thermal Heat Treatment utilizes heavy-duty heaters to bring the entire building up to 140°F, killing all bed bugs, nymphs, and eggs in a single 8-hour session. It is eco-friendly and has zero chemical residue.

Chemical Treatment uses EPA-approved liquid residual insecticides applied directly inside baseboards, outlets, and bed frames. It is highly effective and more budget-friendly, but generally requires 2 visits spaced two weeks apart to target newly hatched eggs.
Yes, absolutely. We back our whole-house bed bug eradication treatments with a comprehensive 30-day warranty, which can be extended up to 90 days with active encasements and monitoring plans. If our technicians detect any live bugs during the warranty window, we will return and treat the affected rooms at no cost to you.
Yes, our treatments are fully safe when our safety protocol is followed. We use state-registered, EPA-approved products strictly in accordance with their safety labels. We require residents, children, and pets to remain off the treated premises during the chemical application and for 4 hours afterward to allow the products to dry completely. Once dry, there is no airborne risk or contact danger.
