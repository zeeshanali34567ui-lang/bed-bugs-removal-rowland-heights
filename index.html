BED BUG FACTS

Important Facts Every Homeowner Should Know

Bed bug infestations spread fast. The longer you wait, the harder the treatment. Here are key biological facts every Rowland Heights homeowner should understand.

Extremely Fast

Bed bugs can lay up to 500 eggs, turning a small problem into a severe infestation incredibly fast.

Expert Hiding

Bed bugs use their flat bodies to hide inside wall voids, mattress seams, furniture joints, and even small electronics - making DIY sprays largely ineffective against deep infestations.

Stealthy Feeders

Bed bug saliva contains a natural anesthetic and an anticoagulant. This numbing agent allows them to feed completely undetected for up to 10 minutes while you sleep.
