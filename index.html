Pest Biology

Did You Know? Fun Facts!

A few crazy, surprising things about bed bugs you probably never knew.

They Don't Fly!

Bed bugs lack wings and cannot fly or jump. They are expert hitchhikers that simply crawl onto your luggage or clothing.

Natural Anesthetic

Their saliva acts as a powerful painkiller, letting them feed completely undetected for up to 10 minutes while you sleep.

Months Without Food

A mature bed bug can survive several months without a single blood meal in the right conditions. Waiting them out does not work.

Up to 500 Eggs

A single female lays up to 500 eggs. A tiny hidden problem in your home becomes a massive infestation incredibly fast.
