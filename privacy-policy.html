Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2023 | Last Updated: March 10, 2024

At Rowland Heights Bed Bug Killers (accessible from https://rowlandheightsbedbugs.com), safeguarding the privacy of our visitors and local California clients is our highest priority. This extensive Privacy Policy document details the comprehensive protocols we utilize regarding the collection, handling, storage, and utilization of data, ensuring full compliance with California state laws and federal privacy regulations.

1. Extensive Data Collection Scope

We believe in transparent data collection. The personal information that you are asked to provide, and the exact reasons why you are asked to provide it, will be made definitively clear to you at the point we request such information. We collect data across multiple touchpoints to ensure efficient service delivery in the Rowland Heights area.

  • Direct Identification Information: Full legal name, primary and secondary phone numbers, email addresses, and specific billing/residential addresses required for our technicians to perform on-site inspections.
  • Property & Structural Data: Square footage of your property, number of bedrooms, flooring types (carpet vs hardwood), and history of previous pest infestations. This data is critical for calculating precise thermal heat requirements and chemical volume protocols.
  • Financial Information: Secure payment processing data, credit card tokens (processed securely via third-party gateways; we do not store raw credit card numbers on our local servers), and billing history.
  • Digital Interactions: Copies of direct emails, SMS text messages to our dispatch team, voicemail recordings, and digital service request forms submitted through our website.

2. Comprehensive Utilization of Your Information

The data we aggregate is utilized meticulously to streamline our local extermination operations and improve our digital presence. We use your information to:

  • Dispatch & Routing: Optimize technician travel routes across Rowland Heights, Diamond Bar, Walnut, and Hacienda Heights to ensure prompt emergency response times.
  • Custom Treatment Formulations: Utilize historical property data to formulate the correct mixture of botanical and chemical residuals, preventing bed bug resistance.
  • Service Enhancements: Understand and analyze how you navigate our website, which pages you visit most frequently, and optimize our User Interface (UI) and User Experience (UX) accordingly.
  • Direct Communication: Provide critical updates regarding your scheduled appointments, pre-treatment preparation checklists, post-treatment follow-up calls, and warranty compliance check-ins.
  • Fraud Prevention: Monitor and analyze potential fraudulent activity, spam form submissions, and ensure the security of our digital infrastructure.

3. Advanced Log Files and Traffic Analytics

Rowland Heights Bed Bug Killers follows a rigorous standard procedure of using server log files. These files log visitors when they visit our website, a standard practice across hosting companies and analytics services. The data points collected by log files include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses indicating regional proximity.
  • Browser types, versions, and device operating systems (Mobile vs Desktop).
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP) and network routing information.
  • Precise Date and Time stamps of each page visit and duration of session.
  • Referring/exit pages and conversion tracking parameters.

Crucially, none of this data is linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The overarching purpose of this data aggregation is analyzing local traffic trends in Los Angeles County, administering the site effectively, and tracking user movement to better serve our local community.

4. Robust Cookie Policy & Web Beacons

Like any modern, optimized website, Rowland Heights Bed Bug Killers uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the specific pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. We use Session Cookies (which expire when you close your browser) and Persistent Cookies (which remain on your device until deleted) to customize our web page content based on visitors' browser types and other local device information.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

Under the CCPA, California residents are granted specific, inalienable rights regarding their personal data. We fully support and comply with these mandates. As a California consumer, you possess the absolute right to:

  • Right to Know: Request that a business disclose the exact categories and specific pieces of personal data that have been collected about you over the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: Request the total erasure of any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected, subject to certain legal exceptions (e.g., data required for tax purposes or active warranties).
  • Right to Opt-Out: Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, absolutely cease selling the consumer's personal data. Note: Rowland Heights Bed Bug Killers does NOT sell your personal data to third-party data brokers.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Be free from discrimination for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

If you choose to submit a CCPA request, we are legally mandated to respond to you within 30 days. To exercise any of these rights, please utilize the contact information provided at the bottom of this policy.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

While our services are strictly limited to the Rowland Heights, California area, we still abide by the highest standards of international data protection. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – You have the right to request copies of your personal data. We may charge you a small fee for this service.
  • The right to rectification – You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to data portability – You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.

7. Children's Information & COPPA Compliance

Another crucial part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We strongly encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. Rowland Heights Bed Bug Killers does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
