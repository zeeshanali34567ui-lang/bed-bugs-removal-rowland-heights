Thermal Bed Bug Heat Treatment in Rowland Heights, CA

Thermal heat treatment is the fastest and most thorough way to eliminate bed bugs. By raising the internal temperatures of your structure to 140°F using specialized heaters and fans, we dehydrate and destroy all bed bugs and their microscopic eggs in just one 8-hour session.

The Science of Bed Bug Thermal Eradication

Bed bugs are highly adaptable insects, but they share a fatal biological vulnerability: **they cannot survive extreme heat**. While chemical pesticides require bugs to cross a treated surface, heat penetrates everywhere simultaneously.

Using industrial-grade, thermostatically controlled heaters and powerful axial air movers, we distribute dry heat throughout the target rooms. The hot air flows inside wall voids, deep mattress fibers, clothes drawers, and ceiling spaces. Once the internal temperature reaches 122°F, bed bugs at all stages (including microscopic eggs) die in under a minute. We maintain the temperature at 135°F to 145°F for several hours to guarantee absolute elimination.

Important Heat Prep Requirements

Because we bring the room temperature up to 140°F, you must remove the following items to prevent damage:

Aerosols & Flammables: Hairsprays, spray paint, ammunition, lighters, and fire extinguishers.
Wax & Meltable Products: Candles, makeup, crayons, chocolates, and oil paintings.
Electronics: Remote controls, laptops, TVs, and tablets containing lithium-ion batteries.
Pets & Plants: All houseplants, cats, dogs, fish bowls, and reptiles.
