About Rowland Heights Bed Bug Killers

Discover our values, meet our certified technicians, and learn why we have been Rowland Heights' trusted bed bug eradication specialists since 2016.

Our Mission & History

Rowland Heights Bed Bug Killers was founded in 2016 with a single goal: to provide high-quality, scientifically proven bed bug extraction solutions that return peace of mind to local households and commercial operations.

Unlike general pest control companies that treat everything from ants to gophers, we decided to specialize strictly in bed bugs. Because bed bugs have built up biological resistance to standard insecticide protocols, we have continuously invested in state-of-the-art thermal heating systems, advanced multi-chemical residual formulas, and thorough inspection methodologies.

We know that discovery is the hardest part. Our certified technicians undergo rigorous ongoing training, mapping out harboring cracks, wooden bed joints, and carpet lines to catch infestations early. We serve the entire Rowland Heights community, including neighborhoods near Colima Road, Nogales Street, and Schabarum Regional Park.

Our Commitments

  • 100% Eradication Guarantee: If bugs reappear within our warranty window, we retreat free of charge.
  • Discreet Service: Unmarked trucks available upon request to protect your family or business privacy.
