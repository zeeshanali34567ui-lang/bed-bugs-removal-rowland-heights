# Local E-E-A-T & Voice Strategy (Rowland Heights)

Based on actual transcripts from Rowland Heights residents, here are the core themes, local entities, and speech patterns we will use to build **Hyper-Local Trust (E-E-A-T)** and defeat generic AI competitors.

## Core Speech Patterns & Tone
*   **Tone:** Warm, community-focused, and grateful ("loved being a part of," "embraced by the community").
*   **Phrasing:** Focus on togetherness ("coming together as a community," "close-knit community").

## Key Local Entities (To Weave Naturally into Content)
These are specific local touchpoints that prove we aren't just a national chain renting a zip code.

*   **Events:** The annual **Buckboard Days Parade and Festival** (This is a massive trust signal. We can say something like: *"Just like the entire community comes together for the annual Buckboard Days Parade, we believe in supporting our neighbors when pest disasters strike."*)
*   **Neighboring Connections:** Mentioning specific connections to **West Covina**, **La Puente**, and the **San Gabriel Valley**. (e.g., *"Serving our close-knit community across Rowland Heights, La Puente, and the greater San Gabriel Valley."*)
*   **Local Demographics:** Acknowledging the diversity of families, schools (elementary/high schools), and local businesses.

## Applying E-E-A-T to the Content:
*   **Experience:** "We've been treating homes in the San Gabriel Valley for years..."
*   **Expertise:** (Using our Semantic Entities: Heat treatments, Cimex lectularius biology).
*   **Authority:** (Using our Factual Stats: "State-certified technicians...").
*   **Trustworthiness:** "We love this close-knit community and are honored to be embraced by Rowland Heights residents. We treat your home like our own."

## The "Anti-AI" Content Rule
No AI writer will naturally bring up the *Buckboard Days Parade* when talking about bed bugs. By weaving these hyper-specific, localized entities into our "About Us" and "Why Choose Us" sections, Google's algorithms will instantly recognize our content as authentic, locally-authored, and highly trustworthy.
