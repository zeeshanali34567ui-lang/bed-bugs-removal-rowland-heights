# Prioritized Semantic Entities for Bed Bug Content

Based on your GMB review data, Harpa AI topical relevance, and the competitor analysis, I have filtered out the "noise" and categorized the **most important semantic entities**. 

To rank in 2026, AI algorithms (like Google's Gemini and SGE) need to see these specific phrases woven naturally into the content. This proves you are an expert (E-E-A-T) and completely cover the topic.

## 1. Trust & Service Entities (Crucial for Local Conversion)
*These are the exact phrases real customers use in reviews when they are happy. Using these builds instant trust.*
*   Thorough inspection
*   Honest pest control
*   Prompt technicians / Punctual team
*   Safe treatments
*   Flexible scheduling
*   Informative staff
*   Issue resolution

## 2. Biological & Scientific Entities (Builds Topical Authority)
*Using these proves to the AI that your content isn't just marketing fluff, but actually understands the pest.*
*   *Cimex lectularius* (The scientific name for the common bed bug)
*   Blood meal
*   Flat bodies / Tiny spaces (Explaining how they hide)
*   Diatomaceous earth (Often searched as a DIY remedy; good to mention why professional help is better)
*   Carbon dioxide (How bed bugs track humans)
*   Young bedbugs / Eggs

## 3. Symptoms & Identification Entities (For Informational Queries)
*These belong in the "Fun Facts" or "Recognizing a Problem" sections to capture people who aren't sure what bit them yet.*
*   Bite marks / Red bumps / Hives
*   Allergic reactions / Skin problems
*   Secondary infections (What happens if you scratch)
*   Signs and symptoms
*   Mattress / Furniture / Small spaces

## 4. Treatment Methods (From Competitor Gaps)
*These entities explicitly define what you DO.*
*   Heat treatment (Thermal remediation)
*   Chemical treatment (Insecticides)
*   Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
*   Pest control experts

---

### How we will use this:
When we write the content, instead of saying: *"We are good at killing bugs,"* we will say: *"Our **punctual team** of **pest control experts** performs a **thorough inspection** of your **mattress and furniture** to locate **Cimex lectularius** hiding in **tiny spaces**, before applying **safe treatments**."* 

*(Notice how that hits 7 semantic entities in one sentence while sounding completely natural!)*
